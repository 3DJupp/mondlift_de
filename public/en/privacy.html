Without consent the site sets no cookies, and apart from the spam protection of the contact form, everything that runs in your browser comes from this domain.

2. Hosting with Cloudflare

This website runs as a Cloudflare Worker and is delivered via the network of Cloudflare, Inc. (101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA) and Cloudflare Germany GmbH. In doing so, Cloudflare processes on my behalf the technical data that arise when the site is accessed. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place through Cloudflare's terms of service.

Pages are delivered from the geographically nearest data centre, usually within the EU. Processing in third countries, in particular the USA, cannot be ruled out. Cloudflare bases such transfers on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest lies in the secure, stable and reliable operation of the website.

More information: Cloudflare's privacy policy.

3. Server log files

Every time a page is accessed, data sent by your browser is processed automatically. This is technically necessary to deliver the page at all. The following is recorded:

  • the IP address of the requesting device
  • date and time of the request
  • the requested address and the HTTP method
  • the HTTP status code and the amount of data transferred
  • the browser and operating system identifier sent (user agent)
  • the previously visited page (referrer), if your browser sends it
  • the country of origin determined by the network

For operation and troubleshooting, request logging at Cloudflare is enabled for this worker (“Workers Logs” and “Traces”). The logs contain the information listed above and are deleted automatically by Cloudflare after a few days. Beyond that I do not store any log data and do not combine it with other data sources. No profiling takes place, and no data is sold or passed on to third parties.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest lies in delivering the site, analysing errors and defending against attacks and abuse.

4. No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no local storage or comparable techniques to store or read data on your device. There is no audience measurement, no analytics software, no conversion tracking and no recognition across visits. Consent under section 25 TDDDG is therefore not required, and consequently there is no cookie banner.

4. Audience measurement with Google Analytics

If you click “Allow” in the notice at the bottom of the screen, this website uses Google Analytics 4, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. The purpose is to understand which pages are read, how visitors find the site and where they leave it again. Before your consent, Google's script is not loaded and no request goes to Google.

After your consent, your browser loads a script from googletagmanager.com. Google sets cookies (_ga and _ga_<ID>, stored for up to 2 years) and receives information such as the pages viewed, the referring page, time spent, approximate location from the IP address, browser, operating system and screen size. Google Analytics 4 does not store full IP addresses. Google Signals and use for advertising are switched off. Google keeps the usage data for 14 months.

Google processes the data as a processor on the basis of a contract under Art. 28 GDPR. A transfer to the USA cannot be ruled out. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, and the transfer is based on the European Commission's adequacy decision. More in Google's privacy policy.

The legal basis is your consent under section 25(1) TDDDG and Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. It is voluntary, and the site works just the same without it. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage so that the notice does not appear on every visit; after twelve months you will be asked again. This storage is necessary for managing consent itself (section 25(2) TDDDG).

You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future. When you withdraw it, the Google Analytics cookies are deleted.

All files of this site, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, fonts and graphics, are delivered from this domain. No fonts are loaded from Google Fonts or other providers, and there are no maps, no videos and no embedded third-party content. The animation on the home page is computed entirely locally in your browser; no data is transferred in the process.

There is one single exception: on the contact page a script from Cloudflare Turnstile is loaded for spam protection. More on this in section 7. On all other pages your browser does not connect to any third-party servers.In addition there is Google Analytics, but only after your consent, see section 4.

External links are recognisable as such. If you follow a link, the privacy policy of the target site applies.

6. Contact by email

If you write me an email, I process the data it contains (your email address, your name if you give it, and the content of your message) solely to answer your enquiry. Incoming email runs through Cloudflare Email Routing; the message is forwarded to my private mailbox.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR or, if your enquiry is aimed at a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. I delete enquiries once they have been dealt with and no statutory retention obligations apply.

Please note that unencrypted email is not a secure means of transmission.

7. Contact form

The contact form lets you send me a message without opening your own email program. What is processed is the information you enter (name, email address and the text of your message) as well as your IP address. The message is delivered as an email to my mailbox; it is stored only there, not in a database of this website.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR or, if your enquiry is aimed at a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. The legitimate interest lies in being able to answer enquiries. Messages are deleted once they have been dealt with and no statutory retention obligations apply.

Spam protection

So that the form is not flooded with automated requests, three measures are used:

  • an invisible extra field that only automated programs fill in and that collects no data about you,
  • a limit on the number of submissions per IP address; for this your IP address is counted briefly in the memory of the Cloudflare network but not stored permanently,
  • Cloudflare Turnstile as a check that the request comes from a human.

Turnstile was chosen deliberately because it works without cookies and without cross-site behavioural profiles and does not set picture puzzles. When the form loads, a script from challenges.cloudflare.com is loaded for this. Technical data is transmitted to Cloudflare in the process, including your IP address, information about your browser and device, and information about your behaviour on this page as far as it is needed to tell humans and machines apart. Cloudflare states that it does not use this data for profiling or advertising. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is the prevention of abuse and spam.

Apart from Google Analytics, which is only loaded with your consent, TurnstileTurnstile is the only place on this website where content is loaded from a third-party server, and only on the contact page. If you would rather avoid that, simply write to me directly at the email address above; the form is not a required route.
